Where to find your details

Service center and biometrics location are different places

Your service center is the USCIS office that reviews and decides your case. You never visit it. Your biometrics location (ASC) is where you went for fingerprints and a photo, and that's all it does. They're often in different states. For example, your case might be decided at the Potomac Service Center while your biometrics were taken in York, PA.

Both appear on your biometrics appointment notice. The service center is in the top-right box labeled "Service Center." Your ASC is listed under "Application Support Center."

Your receipt date isn't on that notice. It's the "Received date" on your receipt notice.

What does the "Code" on my notice mean?

The number in the "Code" box tells the ASC which biometrics to collect. USCIS doesn't publish an official key. This is how immigration attorneys commonly describe it:

How your data is handled

  • No accounts, no emails, no receipt numbers. We only ask for the 3 digits after IOE09. Nothing on the form can identify you.
  • Updating your case: choose "Add or update my case," enter the same details you used before, and pick your entry from the closest matches.
  • Why this is safe: since there are no accounts, every change is logged and can be reverted. Your block and receipt date are locked after the first entry. Unusual changes, like an approved case switching back to pending, are flagged for review.
  • Want your entry removed? Use "Report" on your entry and choose "It's my entry and I want it removed."
